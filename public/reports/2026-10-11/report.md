Expanded documentation review across 48 creative fields, including new cel-animation and Foley searches. This edition completes earlier screened guides and adds newly screened tools across image/video/audio, CAD, accessibility, installations and creative agents. First guides are baselines, not claims that every project launched today. No discovered software was installed or run; quality gaps and separate model/service restrictions remain explicit. Search attempts cover the full configured plan but are bounded, with source gaps disclosed below. All 343 repository and 17 model-task queries were attempted successfully; 103 repository queries remained bounded. Codeberg and SourceHut access was blocked by robots rules. Raw matches are not vetted library entries. The library gains 32 distinct tools; 19 earlier screened entries become full guides. Other unreviewed catalog matches stay private for later screening.

Documentation reviewed today. Tools are not hands-on tested unless explicitly stated. Requirements and performance remain source-specific.
