# AI Media Scout — 2026-10-08

Today’s expanded edition adds practical editing and audio workflows, completes earlier 3D/video reviews, and widens research into neural rigging, textile fabrication and spatial hearing. All 289 planned repository queries and 16 model searches ran across 42 fields. Ninety-nine repository searches were bounded; raw matches are discovery evidence, not recommendations. Forty full documentation guides and seven newly screened leads include a non-GitHub MIT guitar-amplifier experiment. All featured code licenses were read in full, while model-weight and paid-service restrictions are stated separately. Many entries are first detailed baselines for earlier tools, not launches today. Nothing was installed or executed. Codeberg/SourceHut access gaps and unresolved holography, scent and glyph-animation releases remain visible.

Documentation reviewed today. Tools are not hands-on tested unless explicitly stated. Requirements and performance remain source-specific.
