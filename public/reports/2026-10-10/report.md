Highlights span local creative workspaces, synthetic terrain, live captions, AI-assisted CAD, vector animation, notation and research reconstruction. Software licenses are reviewed separately from model weights, third-party assets, proprietary hosts and paid services. Every platform is considered equally; undocumented hardware/platform facts remain unknown. No discovered application was installed or run. All 326/326 repository queries and 16/16 model tasks were attempted; 0 repository queries failed, 0 were partial and 101 were bounded. Live primary-source web research supplements the collector. Codeberg and SourceHut access remains a disclosed gap. Raw search candidates are not verified recommendations; remaining promising work stays in the persistent review backlog.

Documentation reviewed today. Tools are not hands-on tested unless explicitly stated. Requirements and performance remain source-specific.
